# Playing a scene

`PlayGame` (`packages/engine/src/play/game.ts`) runs a scene: the scripts, Jack and the other persons, the insertions, a scene change, the cursor, and the clicks. It has no DOM. The host supplies bytes through `GameFiles` (`packages/engine/src/play/game.ts`). `packages/engine/src/play/files.ts` is `diskFiles()`, a directory opened for tests. `SceneHost` (`packages/engine/src/play/host.ts`) turns interpreter commands into calls on `ScenePlayer` (`packages/engine/src/scene/player.ts`) and holds a macro until an operation ends. Sound is `packages/engine/src/play/audio.ts`. A movie is `packages/engine/src/play/film.ts`. The face window is `packages/engine/src/play/facewin.ts`. The page is the `examples/play` package. The logic does not read the page.

The frame rate is at most 1.5 frames per logic tick. `DONOTHING n` counts frames, so a headless run uses the same ratio. A ratio of 3 to 1 hangs the descent of SC007.

## A scene, the way `Game_Run` `0042b6d0` enters it

1. The scene is loaded. The cursor is the clock (`cursor.busy`).
2. Zone flags reset (`areaState.reset()`, the first 30 enabled). Jack is at `(320, 240)` and **invisible** until the first `SETPOSITION JACK` or `STAY`. The scripts place him. `LAST` is a game variable the scripts set before `GOTOSCENE`. The engine does not set it.
3. `[PRE]` runs synchronously to the end (`Interpreter.runSynchronous`). Then `[INIT]` starts.
4. A frame: the cursor mode from the zones, hits on sprites and zones, the click (zones, then sprites, then a walk), **one step of the macros**, and a scene change if one was requested.
5. A logic tick of 15 ms: `Interpreter.logicTick`, `ScenePlayer.tick` (Jack, the persons, the object animations, the camera). Scene events release macros. The cursor animates every third tick. After the macro step, the frame also takes the first steps of operations that just started (`ScenePlayer.firstSteps`). See [frame.md](frame.md).
6. `GOTOSCENE` remembers the scene and loads it at the end of the frame. With `REFRESHSCREEN`, a `SceneTransition` holds the logic until the dissolve ends. Only two dissolves exist, and only after that refresh. See [transition.md](transition.md).

`MENU` is reset to 1 on every scene entry except the map. The script overrides that stay are the rope descent of SC087, the inert car of SC070, the click rectangles of SC000D, and a click that skips sprites with no macros (the spoon and the table of SC026). See [script-overrides.md](script-overrides.md).

## What the host runs

Movement and placement: `SETPOSITION`, `SETPERSONDATA`, `SETPERSPY`, `MOVEP`, `WALKTO` and `WALKTODIR` (Jack and the persons. A goal outside the walk area is a straight line, [walk.md](walk.md)), `RUNINSERTION` and `INITFIRST` (the scene and `GLOBAL.ICS`), `HIDE`, `STAY`, `STAYDEF`, `SETFRAME`, `SHOWFRAME`, `SETDIRWALK`, `SETCHKPRIOR`, `LOOKTO` and `ROTATETO` (with the turn animation, [turn.md](turn.md)), `GETPERSONX` and `GETPERSONY`, `LOCKWALK` and `UNLOCKWALK`, `HAVEGUN` and `NOHAVEGUN`, `JACKTEMPO`.

Objects: `SETPOSITIONE` and `SETPOSITIONEV`, `MOVEE`, `HIDEE` and `SHOWE`, `PUNDERE` and `POVERE`. Animations: `ANIMA` of modes 1–4, `STOPANIMA`, `CONTINUEANIMA`, `STOPANIMANEG`, `SETANIMAFRAME`, `ANIMADELAY`, `WAITWHILEANIMA`, `WAITTOANIMAFRAME`. See [animation.md](animation.md).

The camera: `SETSHIFTX`, `SCROLLLEFT` and `SCROLLRIGHT`, `SETSCROLLX1` and `SETSCROLLX2`, the margins, `SETSCROLL`. Also `BRIGHTNESS`. Zones: `SETAREA`, `SETSPECAREA`, `IGNOREAREA`, `PINAREA`, `POINTINWALKAREA`. `GOTOSCENE` and `REFRESHSCREEN`.

Dialogs: `TALK`, `TALKRANDOM`, and `TALKRANDOMINDEX` go to `DialogController`. The speaker comes from the third argument: −10 is `LEFT`, −11 is `RIGHT`, −12 is `POS` (the point from `SETPOSXY`). Jack and a person are `ScenePlayer.speakerPoint`, the middle of the sprite and the top minus 10 (`Script_ExecStep` case 10). `-` blocks the macro until `talkEnded`. `+` does not wait. `TALKRANDOM` draws `rand() % count` and stores `RAND`. `DIALOG` opens the menu. The macro runs after the answer has been spoken. The mouse in the menu does not click the scene. The cursor stays visible despite the script lock (`Cursor_EnableDraw`, case `0xb`). Also `SHOWFACE`, `HIDEFACE`, `HIDEFACEAT`, `SETDIALOG`, `EXTRA 6` and `EXTRA 8` (Jack's uniform), and `SETPOSXY`. `IFANSWER` reads `Interpreter.answers`. The same map is given to the controller, which is created per scene. A click, after the button is released (`g_clickArmed`), ends the line on the next face step. The macro runs, and the next line and its voice start at once. The click does not reach the scene, and it does not end the menu. See [dialog.md](dialog.md).

Sound: `EFFECT`, `EFFECTVOLUME`, `WAITWHILEEFFECT`, `UNLOCKCANAL`, `WALKEFFECT`, and `ANIMAEFFECT` go to the effect player. `WALKEFFECT` of a person also fills the person's table. `MUSIC` goes to the music player (`retry` is `repeat`). The scene's music files are read on entry, because the command cannot wait for a file. `MUSICVOLUME` is the multiplier `DAT_008f5dd0` plus the volume of channel 5. Jack's steps and the effects of insertion frames go through the indexed player. `ANIMAEFFECT` goes through the scene event `animFrame` (the picture number is the REC position in modes 3 and 4, the image index in modes 1 and 2, and the FLX frame count in modes 5 and 6, `DAT_006da430`). Speech (`Sound_PlayTalk` `00407750`) is an `.S22` from the scene archive. The channel is `Sound_PickTalkChannel` `00407480` (the first free channel of the pair 1/3 or 2/4. The right-hand branch checks the reservation of channels 1/3, which is an original bug the engine keeps). The volume is `0x40·TalkVol/64`. The voice length sets the line's time (about the length times 1.07). At the end of a line, `Snd_StopChannel` stops only the channels that carry speech (`g_sndChannelTalk`), then `Music_DuckUp(1)`.

**The caption disappears after `strlen·3 + 200` ticks** (`DAT_008ab5e8`, `Script_ExecStep` case 10. `Game_TimerTick` counts every 15 ms, and `Game_Run` calls `Caption_Hide` at 0), independent of the voice. A caption of 59 characters is 377 ticks, 5.66 seconds, and it can vanish while a voice of about 6 seconds is still playing. The engine does the same (`PlayGame.captionTimer`). The line's time (the face, the macro) stays with the voice. See [dialog.md](dialog.md).

A channel named in an effect is a wish. The player picks a free channel.

## Order, and the departures

The order in which operations finish matches `Game_Run`. See [frame.md](frame.md).

- `INITFIRST` (Jack and a person) **does not count and does not wait**. It sets the shared flag `insReleaseEarly` (`0x8f5dcc`, `0041a88c` and `0041aa1e`). The first insertion that draws a frame after that, Jack's first (`00433ce1`) and then the persons in index order (`0043634a`), stops and consumes the flag. The event only sets `runnable` of the actor's macro, **with no `pending--`** and no end-of-insertion event. Jack's slot stays after the last `RUNINSERTION JACK` (`INITFIRST JACK` does not set it). A person's slot is the `INITFIRST` macro. Two `INITFIRST` calls in `[PRE]` collide the way the original does.
- `WALKTODIR JACK` (`0041b7a5`) does not search for a path. It walks straight (`Jack_WalkToPoint`), and the wait is counted **always**. Jack already at the goal does not move, and nothing releases the macro. It hangs until a restart. That is an original bug the engine keeps.
- An animation stopped with `STOPANIMANEG` or `STOPANIMAFRAME` (the event `animEnded` with `reason: "stop"`) **does not release** the macro. Only the end (`"end"`) does.
- The first step of an operation, in the frame of the command: after the macro step, `ScenePlayer.firstSteps` takes the first step of whatever the command or the click just started with a cleared counter (`WALKTO JACK`, `RUNINSERTION JACK`, `INITFIRST`, a turn from `ROTATETO` or `STAY`). A macro released by that step runs on the next frame. That is why `INITFIRST` shows its first frame in the scene's own frame.
- A new walk, and every new turn (a corner of the path, `LOOKTO`), cancel a wait on `ROTATETO` or `STAY` (`00411a08`, `00410909`). The end of the turn does not send `jackTurnEnded`, and the macro waits until a restart.
- `STAY` with a direction changes the direction without drawing. A turn from the old direction waits like `ROTATETO`. With no turn, the stand is drawn only when Jack was moving. With `*`, only the direction. Jack not yet drawn gets the stand at once.
- `ROTATETO` and `STAY` with a turn block the macro **regardless of the flag** (`0041fdfb`, `0041a239`: `runnable` is 0. In the data they have no flag). The interpreter blocks only on `-`. The host therefore waits on the turn itself. In the original, further actions of the scene start only after the turn (`004317ae`).
- Still different from the game, on purpose or because the data never shows it: a line is released before the macro step. `ANIMA` with no end and `-`. `complete` clamps the counter to 0. `ANIMA` of modes 2, 4, and 6 with `-` and a negative pending differ from the original, and there is no effect in the data. A person's `RUNINSERTION` leaves the step counter alone, which is what the original does.
- An operation ends on an engine event (`walkArrived`, `insertionEnded`, the end of an animation), not on the counters inside `Game_Run`. The addressee of the event is the **last macro that started** that actor's operation. An earlier one keeps its wait count. That is the same as the game.
- Draw order: the comparisons are `arrangePerson` and `arrangeJack` on visible sprites whose rectangles overlap. The original uses the list `g_renderTouched` of the last redraw, the union of the old rectangle and the new one. Jack's shadows travel with him. `Sprite_MoveFront` has no limit of `index < count − 2` (the last slot in the original is the cursor, which is not on the engine's list).
- `SETPERSONDATA JACK` with `f` of 3.0 keeps the scale from the moment of the command. `f` of 2.0 sets `refScale` to 1000 (the scale is 1 by truncation).
- Avoiding a person is wired. See [avoid.md](avoid.md). A walk aside always steps Jack to the side, and it stops the `OUTMACRO`.
- Sound after a gesture: from the start screen the audio context is created in the handler of the click or the key, so sound plays from the first frame. Only with `?scene` or `?start=1` (no start screen) the browser holds the context suspended until the first click or key. The game runs at once. Sounds start after the unlock. The original has no such gate. The line's time is the controller's clock, not the sound, so before the unlock the speech is lost and the line continues. A movie before the gesture plays on the nominal clock. A pause (lost focus, a hidden or throttled page) stops the loop, the movie, and the sound together. See [pause.md](pause.md).
- The face window copies the scene under the window and runs it through the color tables, then draws the portrait. It is redrawn only when the face changes, so the background is a snapshot from that moment. `SHOWFACE` waits while the view is scrolling. `HIDEFACEAT` hides the face only after the current line ends. See [dialog.md](dialog.md).
- Music ducking defaults to on. The downward target is a hypothesis. The engine uses half the volume. See [dialog.md](dialog.md).
- Before a movie, the volume falls by 1 every 10 ms, then the music stops. The pause is the volume times 10 ms (350 ms at a music volume of 35, up to 640 ms at 64, shorter while the music is ducked, and zero when no music is playing). The scene stands on its last frame. The engine fades linearly (`MOVIE_FADE_STEP_MS` is 10) and starts the movie when the time has passed. A 50 ms pause after the movie is omitted. The original's argument there is unreadable.
- The cursor over the answer menu. The code of `DIALOG` only re-picks the arrow picture in mode 0, and the cursor animation stands while the menu or a caption is up. **The code does not switch the mode** (a hand from a take, a fist, or an item would stay). In play the answer menu shows the arrow, including after a take. The engine follows play: while the menu is open the cursor mode is 0, and after it closes the previous mode returns. That is a departure.
- `EXTRA 1` (the revolver from the safe, SC014 and SC015) loads the second cursor set and sets `GUN` to 1. It does **not** change the cursor mode and it does not open the menu. The cursor stays the hand, the bullet does not open by itself, and after the player opens it the revolver is in it. The engine does the same (`setGun` only sets `GUN`).
- A line started while another line is running. The game has one slot for "a macro is waiting on a line". The second line leaves the first macro waiting forever. The engine releases it. That departure shows up when a line's time differs from the original (for example with no sound, when the time comes from the text).
- After `RUNAVX`, the second name is stored and `g_sceneChange` is set, but the scene name is never read. The engine enters a scene of that name when one exists, and otherwise reloads the current scene. See [video.md](video.md).
- The verb menu is the bullet, not a row of keys. See [bulletmenu.md](bulletmenu.md). Edge scrolling is wired for the scenes that use it. The city map is [map.md](map.md).
- F5 puts the held item away. The original drops it. See [savegame.md](savegame.md).
- The right-button edge is never cleared. There is no right-button cursor rotation in the executable.
- `AUTOINCRANGE` survives a scene change. The patrol clock uses it.
- Resume after a death movie restores variables from before the movie, which is the original, and it can be the wrong set.

Scroll direction, once set, is not cleared by `Game_Run` or by `Game_Tick14ms`. The view scrolls to the limit until a move the other way changes the direction. The engine does the same.

## Tests

`packages/engine/src/play` tests build their own bytes. They check that `TALK`, `DIALOG`, `SHOWFACE`, `SETDIALOG`, `EXTRA`, `RUNAVX`, `ANIMA`, and `ACTIVEMACROANIMA` reach the right module, that a click ends a line, that a scene change follows a dialog, and that a headless run of a scene does not throw and does not skip a command the host claims to run. They do not open a user's game files.
